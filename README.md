# 🛒 Sales & Revenue Performance Dashboard 

An end-to-end interactive data analytics project analyzing sales performance, revenue trends, regional distribution, and profitability metrics for an enterprise electronics retailer across major Indian cities.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

During the calendar year **2025 (01-01-2025 to 31-12-2025)**, the business generated **₹3.57 Cr in Total Revenue** and **₹1.07 Cr in Total Profit** across **1,131 units sold**, maintaining an Average Order Value (**AOV**) of **₹34.68K**.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue:** ₹3.57 Cr
* **Total Profit:** ₹1.07 Cr
* **Total Units Sold:** 1,131 units (1.131K)
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹34.68K

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights & Breakdown

### 1. Volume & Profitability by Category
* **Mobile:** Highest volume driver with **353 units (31.21%)** generating **₹0.29 Cr** in profit.
* **Appliance:** Second largest volume driver with **300 units (26.53%)** yielding **₹0.26 Cr** in profit.
* **Laptop:** **150 units (13.26%)**generating **₹0.22 Cr** in profit (high margin efficiency per unit).
* **TV:** **148 units (13.09%)**contributing **₹0.06 Cr** profit.
* **Audio:** **144 units (12.73%)** yielding **₹0.03 Cr** profit.
* **Electronics:** **36 units (3.18%)** yielding **₹0.06 Cr** profit.

### 2. Geographic Distribution
* **South Region:** Dominates sales with **513 units (45.36%)**.
* **West Region:** Contributes **406 units (35.9%)**.
* **North Region:** Accounts for **212 units (18.74%)**.
* **Key Cities Covered:** Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune.

### 3. Payment Method Distribution
* **Card:** 360 orders (**31.83%**)
* **UPI:** 325 orders (**28.74%**)
* **Cash:** 261 orders (**23.08%**)
* **EMI:** 185 orders (**16.36%**

---

## 💡 Strategic Business Insights

1. **High-Margin vs. High-Volume Drivers:**
   * **Mobiles** and **Appliances** drive bulk unit traffic (**57.74% combined share**), acting as primary customer acquisition channels.
   * **Laptops** deliver outsized profitability, generating **20.5% of total profit** despite accounting for only **13.26% of units sold**.

2. **Geographic Concentration:**
   * **South and West regions account for 81.26% of total unit sales**. The **North region (18.74%)** presents an opportunity for targeted regional marketing and channel expansion.

3. **Digital Payment Adoption:**
   * **Digital methods (Card + UPI) represent 60.57% of transactions**. EMI adoption (**16.36%**) closely tracks high-ticket purchases (Laptops and TVs).

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

* **Cross-Selling & Bundling:** Pair high-volume products (Mobiles/Appliances) with high-margin accessories (Audio/Electronics) or warranty plans to improve overall cart margin.
* **North Region Expansion:** Run localization campaigns and promotional pricing in Northern hubs (e.g., Delhi) to bridge the volume gap with Southern and Western markets.
* **Financing Partnerships:** Offer zero-cost EMI promotions on Laptops and Premium TVs to incentivize higher-ticket purchases via digital channels.
